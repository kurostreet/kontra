RULES

Registered players receive 3 free attempts every day at 00:00 MSK.

  1. 01

    Use one attempt and press LAUNCH to send the ball onto the field.

  2. 02

    Hold LEFT and RIGHT to control the flippers and keep the ball alive.

  3. 03

    Guide the ball into the green EXIT hole to win and collect the jackpot.

  4. 04

    If the ball falls through the center or either lower drain, the attempt ends.

Targets recharge during the attempt. A maximum of 75 tokens can be credited per run.
